FAQ

Common questions

Is YouthPerks completely free?
Yes, 100% free for all verified students. No subscription fees, no hidden charges.
How do I verify my student status?
Use your university email address or upload a photo of your student ID. The whole process takes under two minutes.
Which cities are covered at launch?
Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Faisalabad and more. New cities added every month.
When does the app launch?
August 10, 2026 at 4:00 PM PKT. Join the waitlist to get early access and launch rewards.
How can I contact support?
Email us at support@youthperks.com for help, general@youthperks.com for general inquiries, or partner@youthperks.com for brand partnerships.
