FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before the launch.

How does YouthPerks verify my student status?
We use a simple verification process through your university email address or student ID card photo. It takes under 2 minutes and grants you instant access to all deals.
Is YouthPerks free for students?
100% free for students — always. We partner with brands who want to reach the student market, so you never pay a rupee to access discounts.
What brands and categories are on YouthPerks?
500+ brand partners across food & dining, fashion, tech, travel, entertainment, health, books, and more. New brands are added every week before and after launch.
How do I become a campus ambassador?
Fill out our ambassador application form. Selected students receive leadership certificates, performance-based stipends, brand network access, and a real-world experience that builds their CV.
Which cities and universities does YouthPerks cover?
We're launching with coverage across all major Pakistani university cities — Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Faisalabad, and more. If your city isn't listed at launch, it will be added very soon.
When exactly does YouthPerks launch?
We go live on August 10, 2026 at 4:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time. Subscribe to our newsletter to receive early access before the public launch.
