Frequently Asked Questions

How does YouthPerks verify my student status?
We use a simple verification process through your university email ID or student ID card. Verification takes less than 2 minutes and gives you instant access to all discounts.
Is YouthPerks free for students?
Yes! YouthPerks is completely free for students. We partner with brands who want to reach students, so you never pay anything to access discounts.
What brands are available on YouthPerks?
We have partnerships with 500+ brands across fashion, food, tech, travel, entertainment, and more. New brands are added every week.
How do I become a campus ambassador?
Apply through our ambassador program form. Selected students get leadership certificates, performance-based rewards, and real-world experience to boost their CV.
